What is a Joint Services Transcript (JST)?

The Joint Services Transcript is an official record of military training and occupational experience for the Army, Navy, Marine Corps and Coast Guard. It includes American Council on Education (ACE) credit recommendations for each course and occupation.

For Veteran Services offices & School Certifying Officials

Who it’s for

Veteran Services offices, School Certifying Officials (SCOs), registrars and military-friendly admissions teams. It covers Army, Navy, Marine Corps and Coast Guard records through the JST, and Air Force and Space Force records through the Community College of the Air Force (CCAF) transcript.
