How LioraAI works

From Any Transcript to Posted Credit in Five Steps

Ingest

PDF, scanned paper, EDI and PESC XML transcripts from any institution

Extract

Courses, grades, credits and terms pulled into a clean record

Match

Each course matched to your catalog using your rules and history

Review

Evaluators approve, edit or send exceptions to faculty

Post

Approved credit written back to your SIS

Explainable by design

A Live Workflow, Not a Black Box

Every suggestion shows its evidence: the matched course descriptions, the rule that applied and your past decisions on the same course. Anything uncertain goes to a person.

  • Your evaluators approve, edit or override every recommendation
  • Confidence levels send edge cases to the right reviewer
  • Student records never train general-purpose AI models

LioraAI is EnlitEDU’s academic record intelligence platform for U.S. colleges and universities. It evaluates transfer, first-year, military and prior-learning credit against each institution’s own rules, supports NCAA progress-toward-degree documentation, and gives students a self-service path to a degree.

EnlitEDU is a higher education technology company based in Cumming, Georgia. It grew out of Anatta IT Solutions’ work supporting colleges through student information system projects, and launched LioraAI in 2025.

First-time students coming from high school: Assess. Anyone bringing credit from another college, at any class level: Articulate. Military records: Veterans & JST. Work experience, certifications and portfolios: Prior Learning Assessment. Student-athletes: Athlete Transcripts. Student self-service planning: Degree Pathway. Transfer analytics: Mobility Intelligence.

EnlitEDU is headquartered at 3840 Windermere Pkwy, Suite 403, Cumming, Georgia 30041, in Forsyth County, north metro Atlanta.
