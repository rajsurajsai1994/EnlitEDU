What is LioraAI Athlete Transcripts?

It is the LioraAI module that helps athletic compliance and academic support staff evaluate incoming and continuing student-athlete transcripts, covering transfer credit, core courses and progress-toward-degree, fast enough to keep certification on schedule.

For athletic compliance & academic support for athletics

Who it’s for

Athletic compliance officers, NCAA compliance directors, academic advisors for athletics and the registrar’s office. LioraAI supports your certifying official and the NCAA Eligibility Center; it does not certify eligibility itself.

Progress-toward-degree (PTD) is a set of academic benchmarks a student-athlete must meet to stay eligible, such as completing a set percentage of a specific degree by certain points, along with credit-hour and GPA minimums. For transfers, PTD is recalculated against the new institution’s degree requirements.
