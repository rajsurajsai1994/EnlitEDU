What is LioraAI Assess?

LioraAI Assess is first-year (freshman) transcript evaluation software. It extracts courses and grades from high school transcripts, standardizes GPA across 4.0, 5.0, 6.0 and 100-point scales with your institution’s documented formula, recognizes Honors, AP and IB courses, and sends clean records to your CRM and SIS.

For admissions directors & enrollment teams

Who it’s for

Admissions directors, application processors and enrollment VPs at institutions that review first-year (freshman) applicants: students coming to college for the first time from high school, homeschool or a GED program. If a student is bringing college credit from another institution, see Articulate.

Which module do I need?

Plain definitions, because community colleges and universities don’t always use the same words.

Students coming to college for the first time from high school, homeschool or a GED (first-year / freshman)LioraAI Assess →
Anyone bringing credit from another college or university, whatever their class standingLioraAI Articulate →
Service members and veterans with a JST, CCAF transcript or DD-214Veterans & JST →
Adult learners with certifications, work experience, exams or a portfolioPrior Learning Assessment →
Student-athletes, incoming transfers and continuingAthlete Transcripts →
