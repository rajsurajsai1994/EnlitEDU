What is Student Mobility Intelligence?

It is the LioraAI analytics module that maps transfer pathways, measures credit loss, flags retention risk and identifies which feeder schools and programs have the most unmet demand, giving enrollment leaders weekly, actionable information.

For VPs of enrollment, student affairs & institutional research

Who it’s for

VPs of enrollment and student affairs, institutional research, transfer center directors and leaders of partnerships between community colleges and universities. It also supports system offices that need a cross-campus view.
