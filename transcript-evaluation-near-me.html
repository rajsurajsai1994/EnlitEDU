Transcript Evaluation Near Me: Which One Do You Need?

“Transcript evaluation” means two different things. Colleges evaluate thousands of transcripts to award credit. Individuals get one transcript evaluated, usually a foreign degree. Here’s how to find the right help, wherever you are.

Where can I get a transcript evaluation near me?

If you are a college or university anywhere in the U.S., EnlitEDU provides LioraAI transcript evaluation software, with online demos in every state and in-person visits on request from its metro Atlanta team. If you are a student who needs your own foreign transcript or degree evaluated, use a credential evaluation service accepted by your college, usually a NACES member; most work online, so location rarely matters.

For colleges & universities

You evaluate transcripts for admission or transfer

Registrar’s offices, admissions teams, School Certifying Officials and athletics compliance staff evaluate thousands of transcripts a year. That is what LioraAI is for: it reads each transcript, applies your rules, and gives staff a documented recommendation to approve. EnlitEDU works with colleges across the U.S., online or on campus.

  • Transfer credit, freshman, military and prior-learning transcripts
  • Any state’s transfer rules, configured for you
  • Online demos in every state; on-campus on request

For individuals

You need your own transcript or degree evaluated

If you studied outside the U.S. and a college, employer or licensing board asked for an evaluation, you need a credential evaluation service, not software. Most colleges require a member of the National Association of Credential Evaluation Services (NACES). Searches like “credential evaluation near me” or “foreign transcript evaluation near me” usually mean this kind of service.

  • Ask your college or board which evaluators it accepts
  • Choose document-by-document or course-by-course, as required
  • Most services work online, so location rarely matters

EnlitEDU does not evaluate individual transcripts.

What is transcript evaluation?

Transcript evaluation means reviewing an academic record to decide what it is worth somewhere else: which courses transfer, how grades convert, and how much credit applies to a degree. Colleges do it for every transfer, first-year, military and returning student. Credential evaluators do it for individuals with foreign records.

How long does transcript evaluation take?

In many registrar’s offices a manual transfer evaluation takes three to eight weeks. With LioraAI, a transcript is evaluated in under a minute and staff typically approve decisions within days. Roane State Community College went from about three weeks to two days.

Does “near me” matter for colleges?

Less than fit. What matters is a vendor that knows your state’s transfer rules, works with your SIS and can meet in person when you need it. EnlitEDU works with colleges across the U.S. online, and its team is headquartered in Cumming, Georgia, in north metro Atlanta, for institutions that want to meet face to face.
