Prior Learning Assessment: A Practical Guide for Registrars

PLA can dramatically improve adult learner and veteran completion rates. But most institutions implement it in a way that defeats the purpose. Here's what actually works.

January 8, 2025 8 min read EnlitEDU Team · Reviewed by Chaitanya Saripalli
EnlitEDU TeamWritten with registrars, evaluators and enrollment leaders. Updated 2026-09-01.
Reviewed by Chaitanya SaripalliFounder & CEO of EnlitEDU and founder of Anatta IT Solutions, which supports colleges’ student information systems. LinkedIn

Prior learning assessment - the process of awarding college credit for learning that happened outside a traditional classroom - has been proven to improve adult learner completion rates by 2-3x. Yet most institutions that offer PLA programs see only a fraction of eligible students use them. The reason is almost always the same: the process is too slow, too opaque, and too dependent on individual faculty availability.

The Four PLA Pathways

Standardized exams (CLEP, DSST): Students take a standardized test and receive credit if they score above the institution's cutoff. Fast, low-cost, well-understood. Best for general education courses.

ACE and NCCRS credit recommendations: The American Council on Education and National College Credit Recommendation Service evaluate training programs, military, corporate, and professional, and publish credit recommendations. Your institution maps these recommendations to your catalog.

Portfolio assessment: Students document their learning through work samples, employer letters, and reflective writing. Faculty review the portfolio and award credit. High administrative load but works for specialized professional knowledge.

Institutional challenge exams: Students take a locally developed exam in a specific subject area. Faculty-intensive but allows credit in areas not covered by standardized tests.

Why Most PLA Programs Underperform

The most common PLA failure mode is not a policy problem, it is a throughput problem. Faculty reviewers have limited bandwidth. Portfolios sit in inboxes for weeks. Adult learners who cannot afford to wait for a credit decision enroll somewhere else or drop out entirely.

The data: CAEL research shows PLA recipients graduate at rates 2-3x higher than non-PLA adult learners. But only if they receive a credit decision fast enough to affect their enrollment decision.

What AI Adds to PLA

AI PLA tools like LioraAI do not replace faculty judgment - they prepare the evaluation so faculty can decide faster. The AI maps credentials to competencies, identifies relevant ACE and NCCRS recommendations, and assembles a structured credit recommendation with supporting evidence. Faculty review the recommendation rather than starting from a blank page. Decision time drops from weeks to days.

Frequently asked questions

Prior learning assessment (PLA) is a process for awarding college credit for learning that occurred outside a traditional classroom - through military training, professional certifications, workforce experience, or portfolio documentation. Common PLA methods include CLEP and DSST exams, ACE and NCCRS credit recommendations, portfolio assessment, and institutional challenge exams.

Military veterans receive college credit through PLA by submitting a DD-214, Joint Services Transcript (JST), or CCAF transcript. ACE has evaluated thousands of military training programs and published credit recommendations. Institutions use these as a baseline, then apply their own curriculum to determine course equivalencies.

CAEL research shows PLA recipients graduate at rates 2-3x higher than non-PLA adult learners. Benefits include reduced time-to-degree, lower total cost of attendance, recognition of real-world expertise, and stronger completion rates for workforce and military-connected student populations.

At most institutions, PLA takes 4-12 weeks due to manual portfolio review and limited faculty bandwidth. With AI-assisted tools like LioraAI PLA, institutions reduce this to 1-3 weeks - giving adult learners faster answers that keep them enrolled.

Regional accreditors require PLA programs to have documented evaluation criteria, qualified evaluators, and defensible decision-making processes. LioraAI PLA generates a complete audit trail for every decision, documenting evidence reviewed, AI recommendation, and faculty approval - designed for accreditation review.
