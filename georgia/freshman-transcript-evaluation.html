Freshman Transcript Evaluation for Atlanta and Georgia Colleges

Georgia high school transcripts carry weighted GPAs, AP and IB courses, Georgia Dual Enrollment college credit and a separate HOPE GPA. LioraAI Assess reads them all and applies your admissions policy the same way to every first-year (freshman) applicant.

How do Georgia colleges evaluate freshman transcripts?

Georgia admissions offices extract courses and grades from each high school transcript, recalculate a GPA under their own admissions policy, recognize AP, IB, Honors and Dual Enrollment rigor, and evaluate any Dual Enrollment college credit for transfer. LioraAI Assess automates the extraction and applies your documented rules consistently.

Why are Georgia freshman transcripts hard to evaluate consistently?

  • Different scales. Georgia and out-of-state high schools report on 4.0, weighted 5.0 and 100-point scales.
  • Two GPAs. The HOPE GPA is calculated by the Georgia Student Finance Commission from core courses with its own weighting rules. Your admissions GPA follows your institution’s policy. Mixing them up causes inconsistent decisions.
  • Dual Enrollment credit. Georgia Dual Enrollment lets high school students earn college credit, so a first-year applicant may arrive with a college transcript that also needs a transfer evaluation.
  • Rigor. AP, IB, Honors and Dual Enrollment courses need to be recognized the same way for every applicant.

What does LioraAI Assess do for Atlanta admissions teams?

LioraAI Assess extracts courses, grades and rigor indicators from PDF, scanned and electronic high school transcripts, converts every scale with your documented formula, applies your weighting policy and sends clean records to Slate, Banner or your SIS. Dual Enrollment college coursework is routed to LioraAI Articulate for a transfer evaluation, so applicants know what will count before they enroll.

Is “freshman” the right word?

Many community and technical colleges say first-year or first-time student rather than freshman. LioraAI follows your terminology. In short: students coming from high school, homeschool or a GED are first-year applicants; anyone bringing credit from another college, at any level, is a transfer student.
