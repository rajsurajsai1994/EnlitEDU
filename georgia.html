What is the best transcript evaluation software for Georgia colleges?

LioraAI by EnlitEDU is transcript and credit evaluation software built in metro Atlanta for Georgia colleges. It reads any transcript, applies your institution’s rules, including USG Core IMPACTS domains and the TCSG–USG general education agreement, and sends staff a documented recommendation to approve. Approved credit posts to Banner, Colleague, Workday Student or PeopleSoft.

Who in Georgia uses transcript evaluation software?

Every Georgia institution that admits students evaluates transcripts, but the work looks different by sector:

  • University System of Georgia (USG) universities and state colleges evaluate transfer coursework against Core IMPACTS domains and their own major requirements.
  • The 22 colleges of the Technical College System of Georgia (TCSG) evaluate incoming credit, credit for prior learning and military training, and prepare students to transfer to USG.
  • Private and independent colleges apply their own transfer policy to students arriving from USG, TCSG and out-of-state schools.
  • Admissions offices across all three review first-year (freshman) transcripts from Georgia high schools, many of which now include Georgia Dual Enrollment college credit.

What makes credit evaluation in Georgia different?

Georgia has rules a generic tool doesn’t know about. LioraAI is configured for them:

  • USG Core IMPACTS. The 42-hour general education curriculum is organized into seven domains, and general education credit transfers domain to domain between USG institutions, even when the courses aren’t identical.
  • The TCSG–USG general education agreement. Listed TCSG courses map to a direct USG equivalent or to a Core IMPACTS domain, so technical college transfers keep their general education credit.
  • Georgia Dual Enrollment. Many first-year applicants arrive with college credit earned in high school, which has to be evaluated as transfer credit.
  • Two GPAs for freshmen. The HOPE GPA calculated by the Georgia Student Finance Commission is not the same as your admissions GPA.
  • Military records. Georgia law directs USG and TCSG to award credit for relevant military training, and Georgia’s Army, Air Force and Navy installations produce a steady flow of JST and CCAF transcripts.

How does LioraAI work for a Georgia registrar’s office?

Transcripts arrive by upload, SFTP or electronic exchange. LioraAI extracts every course, matches it to your catalog and the agreements you’ve configured, and gives evaluators a recommendation with the evidence behind it. Staff approve, edit or flag each course, and approved credit posts to your SIS. Nothing is decided without a person.

Because EnlitEDU is based in north metro Atlanta, Georgia institutions can meet the team in person for discovery, configuration workshops and training, not only on video calls.
